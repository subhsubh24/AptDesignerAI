### Description (max 4000 chars)

```
AptDesignerAI turns a photo of any room into a complete interior design plan — curated palette, material recommendations, and product picks matched to your specific space.

HOW IT WORKS

1. Photograph your room — snap or upload a photo from your camera roll.
2. AI analyzes your space — the app reads your room's light, proportions, existing pieces, and style.
3. Get your design direction — a warm or cool palette, material pairings, and a design narrative written for your room specifically.
4. Browse curated products — furniture and décor picked for scale, price tier, and aesthetic fit. Save the ones you love.

WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT

Most design apps show you generic mood boards. AptDesignerAI reads YOUR room — the paint color you're stuck with, the floor plan, the sofa you're keeping — and designs around it. The recommendations account for what you already have, not just what looks good in a vacuum.

DESIGN INTELLIGENCE

The AI distinguishes between warm and cool palettes, understands material coherence (why oak and brass work together, why chrome and pine don't), and checks furniture scale against your actual room dimensions.

SAVE AND COMPARE

Save design directions, swap between approaches, and share beautiful design cards with anyone.

WEB APP INCLUDED

Your designs sync with the companion web app at aptdesignerai.com — start on mobile, continue on the web.

PRICING

Explore for free — analyze one room end-to-end at no cost, no credit card required. Ready for more?

**Apartment ($29, one-time)** — every room in your apartment: unlimited room analyses, cross-room style coherence, AI mockups, and the full product catalog.

**Pro ($49/month)** — unlimited apartments and projects, client-ready exports, and priority support. For designers and property managers.

**Pro Annual ($399/year)** — everything in Pro, billed yearly. Save 32% vs monthly ($33.25/month).

Pricing details shown at checkout. 30-day money-back guarantee on paid plans.

PRIVACY

Room photos are processed by AI to generate your design plan. Photos are stored in your account and deleted when you delete your account. We do not sell your data. See our full privacy policy at aptdesignerai.com/privacy.
```

repeats "design", "room", "AI", "palette", "products" naturally without stuffing.

## Screenshots guidance (for owner to create — see Track D3)

Needed sizes:
- **iOS**: 6.9" (iPhone 16 Pro Max): 1320 × 2868 px, and 5.5" (iPhone 8+): 1242 × 2208 px
- **iOS iPad**: 13" Pro: 2064 × 2752 px (required if supportsTablet: true)
- **Android**: 16:9 at 1080+ px wide; also consider 19.5:9 for modern devices

Suggested flow (5–7 screens):
1. Hero — the "Design any room" value prop with a beautiful room photo + analysis overlay
2. Camera/photo flow — picking a photo from the gallery
3. AI analysis result — room understanding card, palette strip, materials
4. Product picks — curated furniture grid with price/style tags
5. Saved designs — "your room, transformed" before/after or saved card
6. Web companion — "continues on your Mac" (if web parity is emphasized)

All screenshots should use warm-editorial tones (#faf9f7 background, rust/terracotta
accents). Caption overlays in the App Store screenshots should be warm brown on beige,
never the Expo blue.
